How I take a product from idea to factory

  1. Understand the requirementTranslate customer and application needs into a design brief.
  2. Develop the conceptWork out the mechanical, electronic/control and refrigeration approach together.
  3. Design in detail3D models, assemblies and detailed engineering drawings.
  4. Prototype and trialBuild prototypes and run product trials.
  5. Test and refineTroubleshoot and make design changes until the product performs.
  6. DocumentSpecifications, BOMs, quality documentation, operating manuals and installation documents.
  7. Hand over to productionManufacturing transfer, with brochures and technical sales material for the sales team.
